About the event

State of the Map Kerala is a regional gathering that brings together geospatial enthusiasts, mappers, developers, researchers and advocates of open data from across Kerala. It's a platform to exchange knowledge, share mapping experience, and explore new tools — through talks, workshops, mapping parties and panel discussions.

The 2026 edition in Kottayam is the third regional State of the Map hosted by the community, following Wayanad 2024 and Kannur 2025.

About OpenStreetMap Kerala

OpenStreetMap (OSM) Kerala is a volunteer community mapping the state's roads, buildings, public services and natural resources into the free, editable world map. During the 2018 and 2019 Kerala floods, the community mobilised rapidly to provide mapping data that supported relief and rescue efforts — work that continues today through workshops, mapping events, and collaboration with academic institutions and government bodies.
