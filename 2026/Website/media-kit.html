About OpenStreetMap Kerala

OpenStreetMap (OSM) Kerala is a volunteer community mapping the state's roads, buildings, public services and natural resources into the free, editable world map. During the 2018 and 2019 Kerala floods, the community mobilised rapidly to provide mapping data that supported relief and rescue efforts — work that continues today through workshops, mapping events, and collaboration with academic institutions and government bodies.

About State of the Map Kerala 2026

State of the Map Kerala is a regional gathering that brings together geospatial enthusiasts, mappers, developers, researchers and advocates of open data from across Kerala, with talks, workshops, mapping parties and panel discussions. The 2026 edition takes place on 14–15 November in Kottayam, themed “From Letters to Roads” after the city's two-century legacy as Kerala's literary capital — the third regional State of the Map hosted by the community, following Wayanad 2024 and Kannur 2025.

Organised by the OpenStreetMap Kerala Community.
